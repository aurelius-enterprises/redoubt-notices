Sources and licences

Notices are read from public sources and republished as a list of titles, buyer organisations, countries, CPV codes and deadlines, each linking back to the original notice.

  • TED, the EU Publications Office. Procurement notices may be reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU on the reuse of Commission documents. Source: ted.europa.eu. This page is not endorsed by the Commission.
  • UK Contracts Finder. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Source: contractsfinder.service.gov.uk.
  • BOAMP (France). Data from the Direction de l'information légale et administrative (DILA), reused under the Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0. Source: boamp.fr.

Not covered

Germany and the Netherlands are not covered. The German federal procurement portal disallows automated access and the Dutch portal requires credentials we do not hold.

This page is produced automatically by a rule-based classifier, not by a person. Measured on one blind week of notices (2026-10-11 to 2026-10-18, hand-labelled), it found 0.74 of the defence notices (recall) and 0.93 of what it listed was defence (precision). Some notices are missed and a few listed are not defence; check the linked original notice.
