Privacy and contact

The pages list organisations only. No contact persons, personal email addresses or telephone numbers are collected or shown. Where a source notice names a person, that field is dropped.

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. If a visit counter is switched on, it records the page address, referrer and screen width only, with no cookie and no identifier.

Coverage: TED, BOAMP (France) and UK Contracts Finder. Germany and the Netherlands are not covered.

Takedown requests, corrections and questions: open an issue at https://github.com/aurelius-enterprises/redoubt-notices/issues. Issues are public and read by a human; do not include personal data.

This page is produced automatically by a rule-based classifier, not by a person. Measured on one blind week of notices (2026-10-11 to 2026-10-18, hand-labelled), it found 0.74 of the defence notices (recall) and 0.93 of what it listed was defence (precision). Some notices are missed and a few listed are not defence; check the linked original notice.
